Velora 2

A business ordering and management solution for restaurants. Customers order from their table, staff manage incoming orders, and owners run the business from one dashboard.

Project type
Developed independently by me, not commissioned by a client
Status
Core application built and tested locally. Production deployment isn't confirmed.
Payments
Customers currently pay staff in person. Online card payment isn't implemented.

The business problem

Taking orders by hand slows service down and leaves room for mistakes. Customers wait to be noticed, staff pass messages back and forth, and owners have no single view of what's happening.

The solution

One application with three separate experiences. Every table has its own QR code, so each order arrives tied to the right table, and customers, staff and owners all see the order's progress.

For customers

  • Scan the table's QR code to open the digital menu.
  • Browse by category or search, then add items to a cart.
  • Submit the order digitally and follow its status: Placed, Confirmed, Preparing, Ready, Served.

For staff

  • A separate dashboard for managing incoming orders.
  • Orders appear there as customers place them.
  • Staff take payment in person and mark the order as paid.

For owners

  • A central dashboard for business activities.
  • Manage menu items, categories, orders, payments and staff.
  • Menu changes reach the customer menu as soon as they're saved.
